About Dataset
"### Context\nOn 26 January 2026, WHO reported two laboratory-confirmed cases
of Nipah virus (NiV) infection in West Bengal, India. This dataset captures the details of these cases,
the results of the extensive contact tracing (simulated based on the report's aggregate numbers), and historical
data of Nipah outbreaks in the region for comparative analysis.\n\n### Content\nThis dataset contains 3 CSV files:\n\n####

nipah_wb_2026_cases.csv\nDetails of the confirmed cases.\n* Case_ID: Unique identifier for the patient
(e.g., WB-2026-001).\n* Date_Confirmed: The date the case was lab-confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD).\n* Date_Onset: Date when
symptoms first appeared.\n* Date_Admitted: Date of hospital admission.\n* Age_Group: Age bracket of the patient
(20-30).\n* Gender: Gender of the patient (Male/Female).\n* Occupation: Job role (Both were Nurses).\n* Location:
City/Town of residence (Barasat).\n* District: Administrative district (North 24 Parganas).\n* State: State
(West Bengal).\n* Status_Jan21: Clinical status as of Jan 21, 2026 (Critical/Improved).\n* Exposure_Source:
Suspected source of infection.\n\n#### 2. nipah_wb_2026_contacts.csv\nData on the 190+ contacts traced and tested.\n* Contact_ID: Unique ID for the contact trace (e.g., C-001).\n* Linked_Case_ID: The confirmed case ID this person was in contact with.\n* Contact_Type: Type of exposure (Colleague, Patient, Family, Community).\n* Age: Age of the contact person.\n* Gender: Gender of the contact person.\n* Date_Sample_Collected: Date when the sample was taken for testing.\n* Test_Result: Result of the NiV test (All Negative).\n* District: District where contact tracing occurred.\n\n#### 3. nipah_historical_outbreaks.csv\nAggregated data of past Nipah outbreaks for comparison.\n* Year: Year of the outbreak.\n* Location: Specific location (City/District) of the outbreak.\n* Country: Country (India/Bangladesh).\n* Cases: Total number of confirmed cases.\n* Deaths: Total number of deaths.\n* CFR_Percent: Case Fatality Ratio (Percentage of deaths among cases).\n\n### Acknowledgements\nData derived from World Health Organization (WHO) Disease Outbreak News item 2026-DON593.\n\n### Inspiration\n* Analyze the efficiency of contact tracing.\n* Compare the 2026 outbreak's CFR with historical trends (Siliguri 2001, Kerala 2018).\n* Visualize the geographic spread and timeline.",
